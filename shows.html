Weekdays · 6–10 AM

The Morning Lounge

Jill & Rich Switzer

Wake up in the Palm Beaches with live music, warm conversation and the Songbook's brightest mornings — two working musicians at the top of the day.

Mike McGann

The full music library, wide open — plus Legends at Lunch every weekday at noon, a midday set of the greatest recordings ever made.

Legends Afternoons

Steve Ketelaar

Timeless company from the middle of the afternoon through the golden-hour drive home across the Palm Beaches.

Show page
Weekdays · 7–9 PM

Evenings with Alex Donner

Alex Donner

Supper-club standards for the front end of the night, hosted by the New York bandleader who has played more society rooms than most orchestras see in a decade.

Legends After Dark

Bob Merrill

The lights go down and the arrangements stretch out — late standards to carry the evening toward the small hours.

American Standards by the Sea

Dick Robinson

The station founder's internationally syndicated salute to Sinatra, Bennett, Streisand and the rest — produced in part aboard his yacht Airwaves and carried on 75+ stations nationwide.
